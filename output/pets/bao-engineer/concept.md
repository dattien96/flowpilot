# Bao

Bao is a compact water buffalo engineer mascot shaped around the context available in this workspace: pragmatic software work, automation-heavy flows, code intelligence, review discipline, and a Vietnam/Saigon anchor.

## Design cues

- Species: water buffalo
- Temperament: calm, alert, dependable
- Role: builder/reviewer rather than flashy hacker
- Palette: graphite, teal, warm bronze, muted cream
- Props: utility vest, circuit-pattern scarf, tool satchel
- Style: polished 3D toy illustration with strong readability at small size

## Why this fits

The buffalo gives the pet a grounded, durable presence instead of a generic cute mascot. The teal circuitry and utility gear pull it toward automation and engineering work without relying on screens, logos, or text.
